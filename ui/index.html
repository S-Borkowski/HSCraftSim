Workshop guide

Your first craft

  1. Choose a recipe from Recipes.
  2. Add any required equipment from the catalog. Add ingredients supplies the missing materials.
  3. Check the ingredients in the Cube, then press Craft to combine them. The result appears in the Cube. If it cannot fit, no materials are consumed.

Hover an item to read its stats and description. Click to pin it in the inspector. Drag to move it, or use Move to inventory in the inspector. Right-click removes an item.

Codex crafting

Open Codex crafting from the header. Select one of seven Orb words, add a starting Codex, supply its ingredients, and Craft. The shortcut inserts the Orbs in order. Use the individual Socket Orb recipes for one history record per Orb. The inspector lets you configure a target zone, entries and sockets as a starting scenario. This is not a guaranteed-zone game recipe. Page merges generate a new Codex; Orb socketing preserves its zone.

Recipes for your Cube

Ready recipes appear first, followed by recipes that can modify an item in your Cube but still need ingredients. Unrelated recipes stay visible in a dimmed state. Remove all Cube items to browse the full recipe book normally. Corruption, existing sockets, tier requirements and filled Orb words are included in this matching.

Equipment Runewords

Choose Runewords in the recipe category. The 93 equipment words require a compatible white base, the exact number of sockets and the correct Rune order. Use the full-sequence shortcut or insert Runes individually; each craft creates its own history record. Empty Sockets removes the word. Gold socket markers show enhanced socket effects.

Configure equipment

In the catalog, use the Angelic rarity shortcut and Armor type to find the chestplates. Selecting equipment opens Starting item. Choose your starting properties and add the item. On normal equipment, Starting quality offers a natural roll, Magic/Rare, Mythic, or a Superior base. These are starting scenarios; Tinkerer’s Toolkit keeps the drop quality and rerolls the properties. Advanced includes character level for stats that scale with level; the default is 100. For equipment already in the Cube or inventory, one click opens its stats and controls together. Changes save immediately and can be undone. Under Sockets, search the Runes / Gems / Jewels tabs and click a stone or drag it onto a socket. Recipe equipment buttons show compatible items, with any starting preset clearly labeled.

Fast crafting

Add ingredients places missing materials in the Cube without crafting. Review them, then press Craft for one use. Craft is disabled until the required items are in the Cube. Adding materials and crafting are separate Undo steps; only a completed craft creates a History record. Batch craft uses existing Cube materials only, with one History record per completed craft.

Compare item versions

Last craft shows the first five changes from saved before/after values. View all changes opens that exact craft in History. Expand Recent crafts for the last ten operations.

History saves stats, stars, corruption, Crystal effects and socket contents before and after each craft. Choose two versions to see their differences, or show only changed stats. The last 10 crafts stay with each equipment item, including in inventory and exported sessions. Older entries without stat snapshots are labeled unavailable. Add a copy restores a separate starting item without changing the saved record.

Random results

Every craft draws again. The Orb recipe chooses one of 18 types and produces 8 copies of that type. Repeated types are possible. New sessions use fresh random seeds unless Repeatable sessions is enabled in Settings.

Game data and current limitations

Original Cube graphics, animations, fonts and item images were imported from the supplied game installation. The catalog contains 1,932 named selectable items and 301 recipes, including Orb and Rune insertion shortcuts.

Current-game research supplies all 956 Unique equipment base definitions, their generated stats and natural socket rolls, including Angelic and Unholy items. It also supplies craft mutations, star upgrades, Crystal pools, socketable bonuses, Runewords, Codex effects, and recipe result pools. Random element and gem-specific bonuses are included for the six equipment definitions with those effects.

Attack Damage, attacks per second and Defense now follow the current item tooltip calculations, including socket bonuses and character-level coefficients. Remaining limits include player combat damage, player-dependent effects, Angelic augments, and some socket-content transitions. Face of Existence assumes no allocated subtalents. All 363 normal equipment bases use current rarity, required-level, affix selection, superior bonuses, stars, corruption and socket rules. Special drop-quality and forced socket states also use the current normal-item model. Generated prefix and suffix names also follow the current game. Runewords use the highest required level of their base and socketed Runes. Unique equipment uses its natural sockets even when an older saved socket override is present. Blacksmith’s Mallet only removes sockets from items below Satanic rarity. New Codexes use the native initial draw order for zones, entries, buffs, debuffs and sockets. Eternity Codexes naturally roll up to four sockets; Infernal Codexes up to six. Saved starting scenarios are preserved. Orb word bonuses use the native socket seed; every Orb insertion renews that seed while preserving the Codex zone. Approximate recipes are labeled. This app never writes to the game or its saves.
